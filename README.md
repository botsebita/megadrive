🎮 Botsebita Mega Drive Emulator

The magic of 16-bit gaming returns to your PC!

Relive the golden age of gaming with Botsebita Mega Drive Emulator, a project dedicated to the legendary SEGA Mega Drive / Genesis and the games that defined an entire generation.

Rediscover the excitement of classic games, pixel art, unforgettable 16-bit soundtracks, and those nostalgic afternoons in front of the TV. Built for retro gaming enthusiasts who want to keep the spirit of classic gaming alive.
🕹️ Features

    🎮 SEGA Mega Drive / Genesis emulation.
    💻 Designed for Windows and Online Multiplayer
    🎯 Support for compatible game ROM files.
    🕹️ Keyboard controls and compatible gamepads, depending on the implementation.
    💾 Save features, depending on the capabilities of the released version.
    🌌 A retro-inspired visual identity celebrating the golden era of 16-bit gaming.
